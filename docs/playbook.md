# 7-day playbook: €500 from 150 calls a day

How the lead categories turn into money. All numbers are planning estimates; replace them
with your own after day 2 (Settings → Offers & goal).

## What "promoting" means here

For a small Bulgarian business, being found and chosen online comes down to four things.
Each one is a lead category with a ready offer:

| Need | Category | What you sell | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Can't be found / looks dead online | Broken website, No website, Facebook-only, New business | One-page site: services, photos, prices, call button, map, linked from Google | €290 (€390 for trades, dentists, venues) |
| Found, but paying a platform for every customer | Booking-platform only | Own site with direct booking | €390–490 |
| Found, but the site loses them | Weak / outdated site | Redesign that turns visits into calls | €390–590 |
| Found, but the Google listing doesn't convince | Weak Google profile, No online booking | Google profile boost (photos, description, hours, categories, QR review cards) or online booking | €129–290 |

Paid ads and social-media management (€300+/month in the market) are deliberately not
first-call offers: they need ad budget and trust, which a cold call in week one doesn't have.
They are the upsell once a site is live, together with a €15/month care plan.

## The math

| Step | Rate (estimate) | Per week |
|---|---|---|
| Dials | 150/day | 1,050 |
| Owner picks up | ~25-30% (owner-run businesses answer their own mobile) | ~280 |
| "Yes, send the free preview" | ~8% of conversations | ~22 |
| Pays after seeing the preview | ~20% | ~4-5 |
| Average deal | ~€300 | **~€1,200-1,500** |

€500 needs **2 site deals**, or 1 premium site + 1 Google boost. Even at half these rates the
goal holds. The calling queue is sorted by price × odds, so the first 150 calls are the best ones.

## Daily routine

1. **Morning:** Find leads → your city → 150. Each click searches new business types and
   neighborhoods; nothing is repeated for 30 days.
2. **Call** with Start Calling Session (callbacks first, then best deals). Trades and auto
   shops answer best 9:00-12:00; salons and clinics before opening or 14:00-16:00 — set the
   calling window accordingly instead of the default evening window.
3. **Pitch** what the card says (offer + price + the free-preview question). The opener only
   states what was actually observed ("your site shows a parked-domain page").
4. **Evening:** build the previews you promised (one template per category, 20-30 min each)
   and send them by Viber/WhatsApp.
5. **Next day:** call the previews back. Close with **50% upfront** (€145) to start, the rest
   at launch — cash lands inside the week. Mark the lead **Won**; the dashboard goal counts it.

## Objections (BG)

* *„Имам Facebook, стига ми.“* — „Точно затова звъня: в Google хората виждат линк към Facebook и
  половината не влизат. Сайтът взима съдържанието ви от Facebook — нищо ново не пишете.“
* *„Нямам време.“* — „Затова го правим ние за 5 дни. Пращам ви чернова утре, гледате я 2 минути.“
* *„Скъпо е.“* — „290 € еднократно — по-малко от един ваш клиент. Агенциите искат 1 000+.“
* *„Вече имам човек за сайта.“* — mark *Already has provider*; offer the Google profile boost instead.
